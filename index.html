Category rules at a glance

Junior Division (grades 6–8). Always double-check the official NHD Rule Book.

Every project also needs

  • A title page with only your title, name(s), division, category, and word counts
  • A process paper of 500 words or less that answers five questions: how you chose your topic and how it fits the theme, how you did your research, how you created your project, what your historical argument is, and why your topic is significant
  • An annotated bibliography, with primary and secondary sources listed separately
